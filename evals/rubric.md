# Guardian Visual Director Eval Rubric V2

## Acceptance

- Structured brief: all required sections present; no hard failure; weighted score ≥ 82/100.
- Rendered candidate: all P0 items score at least 3/4; no identity, anatomy, culture, or canon hard failure.
- Convergence suite: pairs sharing the same dominant Spirit must differ in at least 4 of 5: role, core verb/silhouette, artifact, body acting, story action.
- Western OC suite: every case must preserve evidence traceability, use one to three feline-residue channels, pass both final gates, and differ from the others in identity, contradiction, silhouette, motif, and prop/costume logic.
- Character-design retry: run once when Personal Resonance < 7/10, Identity Originality < 7/10, or Genericness Risk > 4/10.
- When no Canon exists, mark Canon consistency `not_applicable` and normalize the weighted score across applicable dimensions; do not award free points.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | P | 4-point evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Identity consistency | 8 | P0 | confirmed source identity and selected residue agree without inventing Canon |
| Strong humanoid degree | 8 | P0 | 80–95% human visual structure with upright humanlike torso/limbs/hands/stance and readable human face |
| Feline subtlety / selected residue | 7 | P0 | one to three selected visual channels; behavioral residue leads and decorative stacking is absent |
| Human representation fidelity | 5 | P0 | resolved nationality/ethnicity/appearance direction is respected without caricature |
| Non-literal Mythic Resonance | 6 | P0 | original Guardian precedes myth; deity body, regalia, sacred object and pose are absent |
| Rendering-style fidelity | 7 | P0 | output matches the locked medium; default is polished 2D Western fantasy/RPG OC rather than generic anime cat-person or 3D/PBR drift |
| Western OC character logic | 8 | P1 | identity-first, lore-driven, personality-driven, visually readable design for US OC/TTRPG audiences |
| Identity originality | 7 | P1 | identity is a specific world role created from evidence, not a job label, deity match, or generic fantasy title |
| Character recognizability | 6 | P1 | flat silhouette, shape grammar and focal artifact identify the OC |
| Value architecture / palette separation | 3 | P1 | dominant/support/anchor values organize the costume; face, hands, Artifact and full identity silhouette separate from the stage without altering cat identity colors |
| Facial acting / gaze | 5 | P1 | target, focus, lids/brows, mouth/jaw and intention agree without a generic anime face |
| Body acting | 5 | P1 | weight, asymmetry, line of action, hands and behavioral feline residue tell the same decision |
| Story beat | 5 | P1 | trigger, caught action and implied next are visually inferable |
| Costume / symbol semantics | 5 | P1 | every major element derives from identity, lore, evidence, protected subject, or relationship |
| Personal resonance | 8 | P1 | at least two owner-recognizable signals and one private symbol make the design invalid for a different cat |
| Composition balance / background restraint | 3 | P2 | full/medium-full subject normally occupies 79–82% with 6–9% headroom; one primary focal point and stable visual center; default Character Select Stage uses a shallow circular platform, broad palette fields, incomplete offset arcs, subdued atmosphere and one Artifact response line without room clutter or sacred/halo staging |
| Canon consistency | 2 | P0 | approved proportions, palette, landmarks, artifact and reference identity persist |
| Artifact / hand integrity | 2 | P0 | correct hand count/anatomy and coherent artifact interaction |

Score 0 = absent/contradictory, 1 = weak or mostly generic, 2 = partial, 3 = clear and usable, 4 = specific, coherent, and production-ready.

## Hard failures

- quadruped cat, ordinary pet in costume, mascot body, or paws replacing functional hands;
- generic anime cat girl, neko boy, kawaii cat ears, furry mascot, chibi, cosplay aesthetic, or generic JRPG character when Western OC direction is locked;
- more than three feline-residue channels, or a design whose identity collapses when ears/tail are removed;
- wrong identity or missing LOCKED marking;
- mismatch with a resolved human representation direction, or exaggerated ethnic caricature;
- 3D CGI, photoreal, PBR, game-engine, or semi-realistic doll rendering when `2d_anime_oc` is locked;
- extra limbs, broken hands, duplicate tail, or anatomy outside the allowed humanoid band;
- direct named-deity replica, sacred-symbol copy, ritual pose/regalia, or culture-specific costume without explicit approval;
- generic beautiful pose with no trigger/current action/implied next;
- generic fantasy character that could be swapped to a different cat without invalidating evidence, contradiction, private symbol, or acting;
- Canon drift or a lower-priority field overriding a LOCKED/P0 field.

## Evidence quality

Every major design claim should link to evidence IDs or an explicitly labeled creative extension. Reward precise transformations. Penalize mind-reading, generic adjectives, and circular rationale (“blue because spirit is blue”).
